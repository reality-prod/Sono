Where Offline Files Are Stored

Sono saves downloaded songs directly to your personal music folder on macOS:

~/Music/Sono/

Audio tracks are downloaded in their original server format without re-encoding, encryption, or digital rights management (DRM).

Downloading Songs & Albums

  • Single Track: Click the download arrow icon next to any song in a list, or right-click and choose Download for Offline.
  • Entire Album / Playlist: Open the album or playlist and click the Download button in the header toolbar.

Exporting Playlists and Metadata

From any playlist or the Downloads screen, click the Export menu button to save your collection:

  • M3U8 (.m3u8): Standard playlist file supported by VLC, Apple Music, and hardware media players.
  • CSV (.csv): Comma-separated spreadsheet with Title, Artist, Album, and Duration for cataloging.
  • JSON (.json): Formatted structured metadata for custom scripts or backups.
