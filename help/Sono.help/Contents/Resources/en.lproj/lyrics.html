Synchronized Lyrics

Sono streams karaoke-style synchronized lyrics directly from your Navidrome library.

How Navidrome Lyrics Work

Sono uses the OpenSubsonic lyrics API (getLyricsBySongId) and standard Subsonic lyrics (getLyrics). It reads:

  • Sidecar .lrc files: LRC files located alongside your music tracks (e.g. SongTitle.lrc).
  • Embedded SYLT / USLT tags: Synchronized and unsynchronized lyric metadata tags embedded directly inside audio files.

Opening the Lyrics View

Click the speech quote icon (quote.bubble.fill) in the bottom playback bar, or press L on your keyboard. Press Escape or click the close icon to dismiss.

Interactive Lyrics Features

  • Frame-Accurate Synchronization: The playhead position is sampled directly from the audio rendering pipeline for lag-free line highlighting.
  • Tap to Seek: Click on any lyric line to jump playback directly to that timestamp.
  • Copy Lyrics: Click Copy in the lyrics sidebar to copy the full lyrics to your macOS clipboard.
  • Export .lrc: Click Export .lrc to save a standalone timestamped LRC file to your Mac.
