Getting Started

Sono connects directly to your personal music library hosted on Navidrome or any OpenSubsonic/Subsonic compatible server.

Quick Setup in 3 Steps

  1. Add your server: Choose Server > Connect to Server… from the menu bar or click Add Server in the Servers tab.
  2. Enter your credentials: Provide your server URL (e.g. http://localhost:4533), username, and password.
  3. Start listening: Sono connects, synchronizes your albums, playlists, and starred songs, and begins streaming original audio.

Core Features

  • Lossless & Original Audio: Streams bit-perfect music files directly from your personal storage.
  • Native Synced Lyrics: Streams real-time synchronized karaoke lyrics straight from Navidrome via OpenSubsonic.
  • Server Management: Connect multiple servers, switch active libraries, and inspect connection status.
  • Offline Storage & Export: Download songs to ~/Music/Sono with no DRM, and export playlists to M3U8, CSV, or JSON.
