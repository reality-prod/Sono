Audio Playback & Controls

Sono plays music directly from your server with original audio quality and responsive native playback controls.

Bit-Perfect & Lossless Streaming

Sono streams audio files in their original format—including FLAC, ALAC, WAV, and high-bitrate MP3/AAC. No downsampling or dynamic range compression is applied.

Playback Transport Controls

  • Play / Pause: Click the center illuminated circle button or press Space / P.
  • Skip / Previous: Click forward or backward or press Right Arrow / Left Arrow. If more than 3 seconds have played, previous restarts the current track.
  • Scrubbing: Drag the timeline slider in the center of the bottom bar to seek within the track.
  • Volume & Mute: Click the speaker icon on the right to mute or unmute, or drag the volume slider.

Queue Management

Press U or click the list icon in the bottom bar to toggle the Playing Queue panel. You can see the currently playing track and all upcoming tracks. Right-click any track in your library to choose Play Next or Add to Queue.

Advanced Playback Features

  • True Shuffle: Randomizes upcoming tracks fairly without repeats. Press Command + Option + S to toggle.
  • Repeat Modes: Cycle between Off, Repeat All, and Repeat One by clicking the repeat icon or pressing Command + Option + R.
  • Playback Speed: Click the speed menu (e.g. 1×) to adjust playback between 0.5× and 2×.
  • Sleep Timer: Click the moon icon (moon.zzz.fill) in the bottom bar to schedule playback to stop automatically after 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
